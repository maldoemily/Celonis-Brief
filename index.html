Automotive · Warranty

UC-3: Warranty & Returns Process Mining

Celonis analyzes warranty claim workflows across dealer networks and OEM systems to find manual rework, duplicate claims, and process deviations that inflate warranty costs and slow resolution times. By connecting warranty data from dealer management systems (DMS), OEM portals, and ERP systems, Celonis creates a unified process view that compliance and service teams can actually act on. Outcome: faster claim resolution, reduced warranty costs, improved regulatory compliance.
